# Seasonal Effects

Light-hearted animations for the seasons, laid over your site on the dates
you choose.

- **Halloween**: ghosts, bats, spiders and pumpkins wander in from the edges.
  Click one to banish it.
- **Bonfire Night**: fireworks burst over the page now and then.
- **Christmas**: gentle snow, and a gremlin who creeps towards a present at
  the foot of the page. Squash him before he steals it.
- **Easter**: painted eggs roll past and hatch into chicks when clicked, and
  Bertie, a black and white Dutch rabbit, hops by. Click him and he does a
  binky.
- **Thanksgiving**: turkeys dash about and flap off in a flurry of feathers.

Each effect has its own dates, with Easter and Thanksgiving worked out for
you every year. Visitors get a small on/off button, and anyone whose device
asks for reduced motion starts with effects off. The page underneath stays
fully usable: the effects sit in their own layer that only catches clicks on
the creatures themselves, and they never appear on administration pages.

Other modules can add effects of their own; see `seasonal_effects.api.php`.

## Requirements

- Backdrop CMS 1.x
- PHP 8.0 or later

## Installation

Install this module using the official Backdrop CMS instructions at
https://docs.backdropcms.org/documentation/extend-with-modules

Then go to **Configuration > User interface > Seasonal effects**
(`admin/config/user-interface/seasonal-effects`) to choose the effects and
dates, and use the **Preview** link beside each one to see it on your site.
